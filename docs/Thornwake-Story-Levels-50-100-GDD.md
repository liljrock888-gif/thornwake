# Thornwake Story Mode: The Unwritten Dawn
## Game Design Document: Narrative Arc and Level Progression, Levels 50–100

Version 1.2 (remade bosses, 10 new bosses, full enemy bestiary) · Story mode · Canon continuation of *The Hollow Crown*

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This document carries Thornwake's Story mode from the end of Act III (Aetherfall, levels 41–55) through a new two-act arc ending at level 100. It is built on existing canon and existing systems:

| Existing canon / system | How this arc uses it |
|---|---|
| The Hollow King (Lv 13), "the last thing holding the dark at bay" | Becomes the central mystery: who he was, and who erased him |
| Five shards of the Hollow Crown, broken by the Shardbreaker | Breaking them ended the cage *and* its jailer. The Hollowing is now unheld |
| The Unmaker (Lv 55), the crown's heart | The level 50 arc leads into this fight. Its last words get a new meaning |
| Malduun, the Demon King (Lv 60), "Rule… better… than I did…" | Expanded into the Level 60 turning point and the Throne/Dawn choice |
| Unwritten Labyrinth, 200 floors, 8 seals | Seal 7 (floor 175, about Lv 50) and Seal 8 (floor 200, about Lv 57) become main story. Floors 201–250 are added for Lv 90 |
| Historian Ilyth: "Someone is tearing out every page on which the Hollow King loses" | Introduces the new antagonist faction, the Blank Court |
| Seal 6: "each small act of care strengthens the ward" | Final resolution: the ward is shared by everyone, not carried by one king |
| 12 classes, 8 races, eye powers, mounts, estates, guild ranks, strongholds | Ascensions, racial evolutions, new mounts, estates and gates |

### Design pillars
1. **Every warden was a person.** Each major boss held something back. Defeating one always costs something.
2. **Memory is a resource.** Late-game enemies erase things: NPCs, map tiles, abilities. The player defends what they care about.
3. **Choices persist.** The Level 60 crown choice changes bosses, dialogue, and the ending.
4. **Cozy between storms.** Upbeat handheld-RPG towns, grassy roads and estates stay the place to rest between dark story beats.

### The one-sentence story
The Shardbreaker learns that the "monsters" they killed were the world's jailers. A secret archive-cult, the Blank Court, erased their sacrifices so that the dark would one day be freed and history wiped clean. The player has to guard the world's memory and then build a ward that does not need a king.

## 4. Milestone: Level 50, "The King's Other Name"

### 4.1 Placement
Mid-Aetherfall, before the Unmaker (Lv 55). Main path: Aetherfall sky-islands plus Labyrinth floors 151–175. Seal 7 sits on floor 175 (floor level formula about Lv 50).

### 4.2 Plot developments
1. **The Broken Halo.** In Aetherfall, the seraph choirs sing a hymn with one word missing in every verse. Players who have restored Lumenreach's crystals can hear the missing word as static.
2. **Ilyth's summons.** Historian Ilyth sends a letter by sky-gull. More pages in the Library of Futures have been torn out, and fresh ink has replaced them. Someone is editing history now, not long ago.
3. **First contact with the Blank Court.** At the Aetherfall Scriptorium, robed archivists are rewriting murals of the Hollow King into a demon. Their lieutenant, **Sable the Ink Knight**, warns: "Put the brush down, Shardbreaker. Some paintings are kinder unfinished."
4. **The descent to Seal 7.** Floors 151–175 are the "Crown's Memory" biome. The maze shows scenes from Aurel Thorne's reign: a harbor feast, a famine, a king volunteering to step into the dark.
5. **The reveal.** Seal 7 opens. The Hollow King's real name was **Aurel Thorne**, first warden of the Vale. Thornwake was built in his name. He drank the land dry because holding back the Hollowing took a life-force, and he chose his own kingdom's over the world's.
6. **Maren's confession.** Maren the Pale admits that her family were court scribes. Her grandmother was paid to forget. "I told you kings always lie. I never told you that scribes do too."

### 4.3 Major conflicts
- **External:** The Blank Court versus the player in a race for the seal fragments.
- **Moral:** The player killed a martyr. Was it right? (No combat answer. The arc treats it as tragic, not as the player's fault.)
- **Trust:** Maren hid her family's role. Her affinity drops unless the player chooses "Forgive" or "Ask why."

### 4.4 Character evolution step: **Shardbearer → Truthbearer**
- The player gains the **Truthbearer** title and the **Lantern Sight** passive: hidden ink-edits on maps glow and can be "restored" for small rewards (12 per region).
- Dialogue choice "Forgive / Ask why / Walk away" sets the **Maren Bond** variable (used at Lv 70).

## 5. Milestone: Level 60, "Crown of the King Below"

### 5.1 Placement
After the Unmaker (Lv 55). This expands the existing Malduun epilogue into a full chapter: the Ashfall Dunes, the Fissure Court (a new underground demon city), and Labyrinth floors 176–200 (Seal 8).

### 5.2 Plot developments
1. **The silence breaks.** The Unmaker's last words ("the silence it was forged to hold… you carried me home") are reread. The Unmaker was the second warden: the crown's silence that kept the Hollowing quiet. With both the Hollow King and the Unmaker gone, only one warden is left: Malduun, the King Below.
2. **Seal 8, the Unwritten Dawn.** At floor 200 the last seal offers "no prophecy." It returns Aurel's lost name to the world. Every crystal rings, but for the first time something answers from below.
3. **The Fissure Court.** In the Ashfall Dunes, the Demon Court is in civil war. Three princes want Malduun's crown. Madame Odile, who knows Court politics, offers to broker passage.
4. **Malduun the reluctant.** Malduun has held the Hollowing in the Below for a thousand years by feeding it his own fire ("feeding a fire with his own hand"). He is dying. He wants a worthy heir, and he will test the player to find out.
5. **"Rule… better… than I did…"** Malduun falls, and the player is crowned with the Crown of the King Below. The Hollowing is now **leaderless and unheld.** Black frost creeps across the Ashfall Dunes.
6. **The Blank Court's reveal.** Casimir Quell appears for the first time in person. He has engineered every warden's fall by editing records so heroes would hunt them. "Thank you, Shardbreaker. Three cages, three keys, and you turned every one."

### 5.3 Major conflicts
- **Political:** The demon succession war. The player picks a side or ends it.
- **Existential:** The Hollowing is free, and someone has to hold it.
- **Antagonist:** The player learns they were Quell's instrument from the start.

### 5.4 Character evolution step: **The Crown Choice** (persistent)

| Path | Action | Gameplay effect | Narrative consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Throne Path** | Wear the Crown of the King Below | Keep the crown's +35/+35/+20 stats and gain **Demonborn**. Unlocks the "Sovereign" skill line. Slow corruption meter in later acts | You become the new warden. Strong but isolated; some NPCs fear you. Ending A becomes possible |
| **Dawn Path** | Shatter the crown in the fissure | Swap the crown for **Dawnshard Circlet** (+28/+28/+24, plus 5% party regen). Gain **Lanternborn** trait instead of Demonborn | No one holds the dark yet. More Hollowing spawns in Act IV, but NPCs rally to you. Ending B becomes possible |
| **Hidden path** (needs Maren Bond ≥ 3 and Odile ally) | "Hold it together": wear it, then give half to Maren | Get half of both reward sets | Unlocks the True Ending route (§8) |

> Existing saves: players who already beat Malduun are treated as Throne Path. They get a one-time prompt in Thornwake to re-choose.

## 6. Milestone: Level 70, "The Margin"

### 6.1 Placement
New region **The Margin** (Lv 62–75), reached by sailing with Captain Aster past "the chart's silver edge." It is a pale land where erased places end up: half-drawn forests, sketched towns, roads that stop mid-tile. Thornwake itself becomes a contested zone.

### 6.2 Plot developments
1. **Thornwake forgets you.** You return home and the square's NPCs don't recognize you. The minimap shows "Unknown." The Blank Court has started erasing the Shardbreaker from history.
2. **The Lantern Archive.** Ilyth, Nyra and Halden set up a resistance archive under the Thornwake library. Players gather **Memory Fragments** (from quests, conversations and restored tiles) to re-anchor people and places.
3. **Maren is erased.** Mid-chapter, Maren vanishes from every save menu and every dialogue line. Her house is a blank tile. The Margin holds an ink-wraith with her voice.
4. **Sailing into the Margin.** Captain Aster's ship takes the player to the Margin's capital, **Palimpsest**, a city rewritten so often that its streets overlap.
5. **Quell's philosophy.** In the Hall of Clean Pages, Quell explains himself: every war, every famine, and every warden's sacrifice was written down and remembered, and remembering made people repeat them. If the Hollowing erases everything, no one can suffer again. He isn't cruel; he's grieving. His whole village was erased by a famine nobody recorded.
6. **The anchor.** The player remembers Maren back into being by bringing her three memories: her first lantern, her apology at Lv 50, and the word "hope." Then they confront Quell.

### 6.3 Major conflicts
- **Erasure vs memory:** Environmental. Areas fade if they aren't anchored, and NPC services (shops, inns) go offline until you restore them.
- **Antagonist:** Quell, whose argument is sympathetic.
- **Allies under threat:** Cedric and Ysra are rewritten as Blank Court knights.

### 6.4 Character evolution step: **Anchor of Memory**
- The player becomes a **Memory Anchor**: their presence stops erasure within a radius.
- New system, **Remembrance**. Every NPC the player has spoken to gives +1 Remembrance (with a cap). Remembrance adds a small resistance to "Erase" debuffs. This rewards players who talked to the town.
- The Throne Path player's corruption meter rises 25% here. The Dawn Path player gets "Rallying Light" (NPC allies join some fights).

## 7. Milestone: Level 90, "Hollowdeep"

### 7.1 Placement
Below floor 200: **the Undercroft of Names**, Labyrinth floors 201–250 (Lv 76–92). Five biomes of ten floors each. Surface side content continues across the world, where Hollowing outbreaks hit the capitals.

### 7.2 Plot developments
1. **Quell's retreat.** Wounded, Quell fled into the Hollowing. The Labyrinth's 200th floor cracks open onto a stairway with no number.
2. **The Undercroft.** Every name ever erased is stored here as a dim lantern. Nyra realizes the handwriting she found at Seal 5 is hers because the Blank Court has been using her future self as its scribe through the Labyrinth's time-folds.
3. **The first wardens.** Floor 220 holds the Nameless Host: the first wardens who gave up their clan names (Seal 4). They have held the deepest door for millennia, and they are almost spent.
4. **Your shadow.** Floor 240 is the Hall of Mirrors. The Hollowing takes the shape of the Shardbreaker: every skill, every choice, every boss killed. It argues that you are just another warden-killer, and the next cage.
5. **The Hollowing made flesh.** At floor 250, Quell has merged with the Hollowing to give it a voice. Defeating it doesn't end the dark. It shows what lies past it: the Quiet Before Names.
6. **Quell's choice.** Freed from the merged form, a dying Quell sees his village's name in the Undercroft lanterns: it was never erased, only forgotten. He can be redeemed (Remembrance ≥ 60 and Sable defected) or he fades.

### 7.3 Major conflicts
- **Internal:** The player against their own shadow, and what the player's history means.
- **Sacrifice:** The Nameless Host asks the player to take their place as warden. The player can refuse, which matters for the endings.
- **Ally crisis:** Nyra has to destroy her future scribe-self, which could erase her present self. The player decides how to help.

### 7.4 Character evolution step: **Shadow Reconciled**
- After the Mirror Shardbreaker, choose **Absorb** (take the shadow's strength: +damage, more corruption) or **Accept** (understand it: +defense, a cleared corruption meter, unlocks the "Dual Echo" skill).
- **Mythic Eye Awakening** with Seer Vael: the eye power evolves to show "the version of each enemy that could have been." This unlocks a weak point on every Lv 80+ boss.

## 8. Milestone: Level 100, "The Unwritten Dawn"

### 8.1 Placement
**The Quiet**, a region outside the map entirely (Lv 93–100), plus a simultaneous siege on every capital: Thornwake, Saltmere, Port Halcyon, Rougemarais, Valdris, Aetherfall, Lumenreach, Cinderward and Veilwood.

### 8.2 Plot developments
1. **The Quiet rises.** The Quiet Before Names is the silence before the world had words. Every warden held back a piece of it. With all of them gone, it starts to un-name the world: towns lose their names on the map, then their colors, then their tiles.
2. **The Night of Every Lantern.** The player organizes a defense. Allies they saved hold the capitals (Royal Watch in Thornwake, Sorrowmere in Rougemarais, Sable at the Scriptorium, Odile with the Velvet Lantern). Each ally you saved earlier means one less capital you must defend yourself.
3. **The Echo Gauntlet.** To reach the Quiet, the player walks through echoes of every warden they defeated: the Hollow King, Ignaroth, the Unmaker and Malduun. Each echo asks "Will you hold it, as I did?"
4. **The Quiet Before Names.** It has no face. It is the absence of every name you have restored.
5. **The resolution.** The answer from Seal 6 comes back: the cities are one great ward, and each small act of care strengthens it. The player lights every restored street crystal at once, so the ward is held by everyone and not by any single king.
6. **Restoring the name.** Aurel Thorne's name is carved into the Thornwake square lantern. Thornwake finally knows who it was named after.

### 8.3 Major conflicts
- **Existential:** Will the world need a jailer forever?
- **Legacy:** The Throne Path player is tempted to become the eternal warden. The Dawn Path player has to persuade the world to share the burden.
- **Final:** The Quiet itself.

### 8.4 Character evolution step: **First of the Many / Last Warden**

## 13. Implementation Notes (Thornwake Codebase)

- **Chapters:** Continue `d.ch` from 9: ch 9 = Fissure Court/Malduun, 10–11 = Margin, 12–13 = Undercroft, 14 = Quiet. Put the Lv 50 arc inside ch 8 as sub-flags (`seal7`, `truthbearer`) so existing ch 8 saves keep working.
- **Existing saves:** `stActSkip()` already moves players past completed chapters. Players with `flags.demonking` start at ch 10 with `crownPath = 'throne'` and get the one-time re-choose prompt.
- **Enemy data:** Add the §12 enemies to `ST_FOES` with region spawn pools (the `pool` arrays on each map). Elites reuse the same entry with `elite:true` and ×2.5 HP.
- **Boss data:** Add entries to `ST_FOES`, `ST_BOSS_LV`, the boss-skill table (the `demonking: [['Hellfire Decree'…` format) and `ST_FOE_SEX` for voice lines. Add death lines to the dying-words table.
- **Labyrinth:** Extend `fwBuildFloor` from 200 to 300, and add biomes 9–12 to the theme and guardian name arrays. The live formula `level = 1 + floor(n × 0.28)` gives about Lv 57 at floor 200. Floors 201–250 need a steeper curve (about +0.32/floor) to reach Lv 90 at floor 250.
- **Objective tracker:** Add `stObjective()` cases for ch 9–14 so the gold chevron points to each new main boss.
- **Music:** New tracks: "Fissure Court" (minor-key march), "The Margin" (a sparse triangle melody with gaps), "Hollowdeep" (noise-channel pulse), and the finale's layered track that adds channels back one at a time.
- **Safety:** All of this is additive content. No maintenance flag, player database or payment config should change. Ship through GitHub to the live Vercel deploy as usual.
