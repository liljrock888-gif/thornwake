On a phone or tablet, drag the joystick to move, tap Attack, and tap an ability to cast it at the nearest enemy. You can also tap the ground or an enemy. On a TV or console web browser (such as Edge on Xbox), plug in a controller and turn on Big screen mode.

Every level makes your hero stronger and adds to their look. At levels 6, 11 and 16 they evolve into Adept, Ascendant and Mythic forms, each with a big stat boost. Bots level up the same way. Minions evolve every 4 minutes for both teams. Each drake you slay gives your whole team +6% damage.

Pets follow your hero, shoot at nearby enemies and give a small bonus. Unlock them in the Store under Pets, then equip one.

Game modes: Conquest (destroy the core), Team Deathmatch (first to 15 kills), Hold the Spot (hold the center circle for 5 minutes in total) and Capture the Flag (first to 3 captures). Every match earns Crowns and Game Pass XP to spend in the Store on heroes, moves, boosts and cosmetics.
