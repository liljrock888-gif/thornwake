Refund Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

The short version: if you ask within 14 days and haven't used what you bought, we'll refund it. We'll also always fix or refund a purchase that didn't arrive, was charged twice, or wasn't made by you.

When you can get a refund

We'll refund a purchase if you contact us within 14 days of buying it and:

  • You haven't used it. For Crowns, the Crowns from that purchase haven't been spent. For Game Pass Premium, you haven't claimed any premium rewards.
  • It was an accident, such as buying the wrong pack, as long as it's unused.

We'll refund at any time, not just within 14 days, if:

  • You never received it. You paid, but the items didn't arrive and we can't deliver them.
  • You were charged twice for the same purchase.
  • You didn't make the purchase, for example a child bought something without a parent's permission, or your card was used without your permission.

When we can't give a refund

  • Crowns that have already been spent, or Game Pass rewards that have already been claimed, except in the cases listed above.
  • Requests made more than 14 days after the purchase, except in the cases listed above.
  • Items earned for free in the game.

If the law where you live gives you more rights than this policy, you get those rights.

How to ask for a refund

Email joshuarosario84@outlook.com and include:

  • the email address you used at checkout,
  • the date and amount of the purchase (it's on your Stripe receipt),
  • your Purchase ID from the Buy Crowns screen in the game,
  • a short note on what happened.

We'll reply within 5 business days. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. Your bank or card provider usually takes 5 to 10 business days to show it.

What happens after a refund

When we refund a purchase, we remove the refunded items from your game. Unspent Crowns from that purchase are taken back, and a refunded Game Pass returns to the free track.

Please contact us before disputing a charge

If something's wrong, email us first. We can usually sort it out quickly. If a payment is disputed with your bank (a chargeback), we may remove the items from that purchase while the dispute is open.

Questions? Email joshuarosario84@outlook.com. See also our Terms of Sale.
